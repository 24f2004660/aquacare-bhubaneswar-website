Why RO service matters

Your RO cleans water in stages. The sediment filter stops sand and rust. The carbon filters reduce smell and taste. The RO membrane takes out dissolved salts. In many purifiers a UV lamp is the last step. When any one of these gets old, the water quality and the flow can suffer.

We suggest getting your purifier serviced every 3 to 6 months, or as the maker of your purifier advises. In some areas the water carries more rust, dirt or salts, and filters there may need to be changed sooner. If it has been more than 6 months since your last service, it is time to call us.
